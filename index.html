About this data. Book lists come from the University Co-op's Fall 2026 adoption list (the official UT book list), pulled October 13, 2026. Prices are Co-op online prices for the exact ISBN, or VitalSource eTextbook prices when the Co-op doesn't sell the item. Syllabus excerpts come from UT's public Syllabi & CVs archive and Simple Syllabus.

Estimated required cost adds the lowest listed price of each item marked Required, Longhorn Textbook Access, or "attend class before buying". Optional, recommended and alternative items are left out, and so are coursepacks sold by Jenn's Copies or UT Document Solutions, which are shown separately.

Longhorn Textbook Access (LTA) items are billed through Canvas, and their real price appears only in your Canvas "My Textbooks" tab. UT says the LTA price is the lowest available, so the VitalSource price shown here is an upper bound.

Unofficial student project, not affiliated with UT Austin or the University Co-op. Book lists change during the semester, so check your syllabus or Canvas before you buy.
